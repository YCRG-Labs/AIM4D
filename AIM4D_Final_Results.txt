======================================================================
CAUSALITY: SPATIAL DIFFUSION, CORRELATIONAL NOT CAUSAL
======================================================================

We interrogated whether the network decomposition reflects a causal spillover.

STRUCTURAL ESTIMATE (robustness/causal_real_data.py), outcome = factor_1:
  Over-controlled level spec (own-lag phi=0.94 + year FE): alpha=+0.005, p=0.17
    -> null BY CONSTRUCTION (Franzese-Hays / Plumper-Neumayer: FE + near-unit-
       root LDV absorb the diffusion wave; an over-controlled artifact)
  Literature-standard change spec (dY, contemporaneous W*dY, no year FE;
  Leeson-Dean 2009): alpha = +0.311, t=4.74, p=2.2e-6  <- significant diffusion
    with year FE: alpha = +0.174, t=2.93, p=0.003 (FE absorb ~44%, the global wave)
  Node-permutation placebo on W: real network in the tail, p=0.004
  2SLS (instrument contemporaneous W*dY with predetermined W*y_lag): alpha=-0.31,
    CI [-1.16, +0.23] -> WEAK-INSTRUMENT blowup; clean causal coefficient
    unattainable.

VERIFICATION: SymPy (robustness/verify_proposition_sympy.py) confirms the
algebra exactly (reduced form (I-aW)^-1, lagged-orthogonality iff serially-
uncorrelated errors, contemporaneous reflection bias). Monte Carlo
(robustness/identification_montecarlo.py) confirms recovery under assumptions
(bias->0, RMSE 0.014->0.005, coverage ~0.95; reflection biases to 0.22;
learned-W gives a partial-ID spread). Lean/Coq formal verification was
correctly rejected as an over-promise for an asymptotic econometric result.

CONCLUSION: there is a real, statistically significant spatial DIFFUSION effect
consistent with the established literature, but it is SPATIAL-CORRELATIONAL, not
experimentally causal (no valid instrument, untestable neighborhood
unconfoundedness). NO causal-identification theorem is claimed. The network
decomposition is a predictive attribution; the dominant transmission channel is
global/regional comovement, not pure contiguity (Schmotz-Selvik 2025).
