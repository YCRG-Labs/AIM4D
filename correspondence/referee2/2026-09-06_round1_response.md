1. **Registration dates.** Correct: the window opens in January 2026, so the 17 May 2026 registration and the 6 September 2026 amendment both fall inside its first year. The Section 7 passage now says so for both dates, applies the knowledge caveat to both (no coded 2026 outcomes in any named source at either date; no claim of ignorance of 2026 events before either date), and reads both as forecasts of 2027-2031 with 2026 partly observed. Both files resolve at github.com/YCRG-Labs/AIM4D (raw and blob, HTTP 200 checked 2026-09-06); the data availability paragraph now gives direct URLs for each.

2. **Country-held-out refit.** The contradiction was real in v26 and is resolved by rebuilding, not by rewording. The audit found that Stage 4 ignored `AIM4D_EXCLUDE_COUNTRY`; that was fixed (`stage4_nscm/estimate.py`, training mask), and the fifteen-episode refit was rerun on the corrected pipeline (cascade log shows the Stage 4 exclusion mask per episode). Limitations, Appendix A and Appendix E now describe the same refit: the held-out country is excluded from every stage that pools across countries (factor extraction, regime classifier, network model, meta-learner); Stage 2 betas are country-local on factors fit without the country, with only their winsorization bounds being training-panel percentiles. The refit raises held-out risk by +0.093 (fourteen of fifteen higher), replacing +0.081 in all three places.

3. **Size-matched arm.** Adopted. Section 5.2 now says that about four-fifths of the closure contrast, 0.154 of 0.193, remains after matching training-set sizes; that matching controls row count and nothing else (recency, composition and label overlap remain); and that the placebo, not the matched arm, is what points to label overlap. The 80/20 assignment to memorization and sample size is withdrawn. Passport C36 reworded.

4. **Power wording.** The categorical sentence is gone. The paragraph now states the approximation (normal interval, 80 percent power, two-sided 5 percent, MDE = 2.8 SE with SE = width/3.92) and separates two quantities. The single-block minimum detectable contribution (0.041 ledger, 0.033 ERT) is at or below the 0.042 the earlier unseeded run attributed to mobilization, so that contribution was powered. The between-block contrast was bootstrapped directly (`robustness/channel_contrast.py`, reusing the ablation scorers): mobilization minus digital control is -0.009 [-0.055, +0.035] at h=5 on the ledger and -0.001 [-0.050, +0.046] under ERT, with minimum detectable contrasts of 0.064 and 0.068 (h=2: 0.077 and 0.053). The 0.035 gap the earlier run implied lies below every minimum, so the strict design was not powered to rank the blocks; the paper now says the ordering is not a claim under either design, and the introduction's power sentence carries the same limit.
