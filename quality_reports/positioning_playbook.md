# Positioning and restructuring playbook (2026-07-11)
Grounded in two live-research sweeps: (A) how 8 canonical adjudication papers framed
a horse race as a first-rank contribution; (B) how 6 ML-heavy papers structured methods
at substantive venues. Sources in the agent reports; key receipts inline.

## A. The six adjudication framing moves, mapped to our paper

1. Stack the test against yourself (Meese-Rogoff: structural models lose "despite...
   actual realized values of future explanatory variables").
   STATUS: HAVE. Refit-CV carried as primary estimate; booster given all 332 raw
   indicators including leakage-prone ones and its raw win reported.
2. Continuity with the field's founding ambition (Hill & Jones APSR: "Our goal is
   essentially the same as Poe and Tate (1994)").
   STATUS: ADDED 2026-07-11. Intro now: "The question is not an import from machine
   learning; it is the founding ambition of the instability-forecasting tradition
   itself, brought to a class of regime change that tradition was not built to see."
3. Scope the negative result narrowly (Welch-Goyal: "we wish to point out what our
   paper does not do").
   STATUS: HAVE. Digital-control claim scoped to onset-within-democracies, not
   consolidation of autocracies (8.1); decomposition scoped as predictive attribution.
4. Make theory-vs-prediction itself the tested hypothesis (Blair-Sambanis: "treat the
   value-added of theory for prediction not as an assumption but as a hypothesis").
   STATUS: HAVE. Section 2.3's paired conditionals ("If the leading instrument...
   should rank among the strongest out-of-sample predictors; if symptoms... should
   prove redundant") are exactly this move.
5. Pair adjudication with a durable artifact (Colaresi-Mahmood software; Goyal-Welch
   maintained dataset).
   STATUS: HAVE + ELEVATED. Registered 2026-2031 forecast with falsification rule,
   now a named contribution in the intro.
6. Invert the burden of proof (Cranmer-Desmarais: declining predictive validation
   "involves the rather bold claim that... our powers of observation are so keen").
   STATUS: ADDED 2026-07-11. Section 2.3 now: "The alternative is to let theoretical
   centrality stand in for evidence of ex ante signal, which asks more of theory than
   any account of contemporary autocratization has yet earned."

Reception meta-finding: adjudication papers get attacked on the merits (Campbell-
Thompson vs Goyal-Welch; Beger-Morgan-Ward vs Blair-Sambanis), not dismissed as
confirmatory. Provoking a technical rebuttal is this genre's marker of first-rank
status. Cover letter should claim the Ward 2010 lineage explicitly.

## B. The venue-translation recipe (from Boese/Cederman/Barbera/Bonikowski/Mueller-Rauh)

1. Equation budget 0-2 in main text (Boese at Democratization: ZERO equations for a
   bivariate probit with selection). Ours currently: 4 numbered equations in Sec 4
   (TVP, inverse-MSE weight, reclassification threshold, contagion score) → all move
   to appendix/supplement.
2. Name the method, cite the source, skip the derivation ("a method introduced by
   Blei, Ng and Jordan (2003)"). We partially do this; make it uniform.
3. Intuition or worked example before any formalism (Mueller-Rauh's sports-words
   example; Cederman's Yugoslavia walk-through replacing the estimator entirely).
   Our Hungary beta narrative is the natural worked example; lead with it.
4. Route robustness/model-selection OUT of the PDF to the online supplement, not just
   an appendix (Bonikowski's ASR editing visibly relocated the BIC table between
   draft and final). Democratization publishes supplements via Figshare, unlimited.
5. Every technical choice gets a substantive gloss: Boese's "to capture X, we use Y /
   to account for X, we add Y" construction, never a statistics-literature gloss.
6. Figures substitute for math where visualizable (Cederman's maps; our Figure 1
   pipeline schematic + Figure 5 betas already do this work).
